Add to home screen

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open the guide from your home screen, tap Turn on reminders, then Allow.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap Install when Chrome asks, or open the ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen.
  2. Tap Turn on reminders, then Allow.
